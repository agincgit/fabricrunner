# Licensing policy

## Current license

AgInc is the licensor of Fabric Runner. Every version is made available under
the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, MIT Future License
(`FSL-1.1-MIT`). The complete controlling terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Before its future-license date, a version is Fair Source and source-available.
It is not described as Open Source. The SPDX identifier is `FSL-1.1-MIT`.

## Two-year conversion

Each version receives an irrevocable MIT license on the second anniversary of
the date AgInc makes that version available. A pushed commit is a version for
this purpose, whether or not it is also tagged. Updating the license for a
later version does not change the grant or conversion date of an earlier
version.

Release tags and GitHub releases identify their publication date and calculated
MIT effective date. [RELEASES.md](RELEASES.md) is the human-readable release
record; Git and GitHub timestamps remain the authoritative publication
evidence for individual commits.

## Contributions

Intentional contributions are accepted under the repository's
`FSL-1.1-MIT` terms, including its future MIT grant. Contributors retain their
copyright. The sign-off and representation requirements are in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

This inbound-equals-outbound policy does not give AgInc a separate right to
relicense a contributor's work. A different license for a future release may
therefore require contributor consent or exclusion of affected contributions.

## v1.0 review

Before publishing v1.0, AgInc will record a license decision that considers:

1. continuing `FSL-1.1-MIT`;
2. using `FSL-1.1-ALv2` for explicit future patent terms; and
3. adopting an OSI-approved license immediately.

That review affects only versions not yet published. It cannot revoke or delay
an existing version's future MIT license.
